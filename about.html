ABOUT

A little about me.

I'm Rikad Rahman, a software developer living in Mirpur, Dhaka. I started with computers long before they were part of everyday life here, and I have been learning ever since.

Most of my work has been with Macs — installing, repairing and supporting them in studios and homes — and with music and video technology. I also build apps for Android and iOS, and websites.

I like finding ways to do more with limited resources: Hackintosh machines that gave creators a professional Mac workflow on a small budget, and a website served from a home router. If it can be made to work, I want to try it.
